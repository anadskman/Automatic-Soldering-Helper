# Quick Assembly guide.


adding this as request because of so many little parts

## Arm

Probably the easiest part, where you see those small holes you just screw in the servos.
using the screws that come with the servos.
to attach the horn to next piece you put it in place connect to the servo and put the horn screw in and one(or two, its up to you) m1 i believe it is.



## Base

for the motor its pretty simple you just place it in and then screw it all together.

the arm mount just uses m4 screws to connected to the shoulder



## Clamp

id just ignore the first clamp and just focus on v4(prints quicker and looks nicer)

the arms just screw into the base and jaw, then the gears go into the base and jaws.

Please note when attaching the servo to put the horn side away from the other gear so they can connect properly



## PCB

lastly the pcb, once its printed you should be able to place the wires into the gaps.
for the case you can leave the side off, or just superglue it there.
